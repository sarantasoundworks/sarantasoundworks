Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 29, 2026

At Saranta Soundworks, we value your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle information when you use our web-based audio tools and services.

1. Data Processing & Audio Files

Our Mastering Suite and audio tools process your files locally within your browser. Your audio files are never uploaded, stored, or processed on our servers. All DSP (Digital Signal Processing) happens on your local machine using the Web Audio API.

2. Advertising & Cookies

We may use third-party advertising companies (such as Google AdSense) to serve ads when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies to provide advertisements about goods and services of interest to you.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

3. Information You Provide

If you contact us via the contact form, we collect your name and email address solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell or share this information with third parties.

4. Analytics

We may use basic analytics tools to understand site traffic and improve our tools. This data is anonymous and does not identify individual users.

5. Contact

For any questions regarding this policy, please reach out via our contact section on the main page.

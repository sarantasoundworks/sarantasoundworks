Saranta
Tools

A growing hub of browser-based audio utilities and plugins built for heavy music production. Open any tool and start working — no install, no DAW required.

Browser App
Saranta Mastering Suite
A full mastering-grade signal chain inside your browser. 8-band parametric EQ with dynamic EQ and Auto EQ, tape saturator, compressor, multiband compressor, clipper, stereo imager, and ITU-R BS.1770-4 LUFS meter. Drag-and-drop reorderable module rack with per-module bypass and WAV export.
Open Mastering Suite ↗
Browser App
BeatMaster
Browser-based step sequencer and rhythm game. 16-step grid with 4 channels, up to 4 bars, adjustable BPM, preset grooves, and MIDI export. Includes a game mode with combos, multipliers, and a global leaderboard.
Open BeatMaster ↗
Browser App
MasterMind
One-click automated mastering pipeline. Upload any audio file and get a broadcast-standard -14 LUFS master in seconds. Includes A/B monitoring, fine-tune 3-band EQ, and a full mastering signal chain.

Saranta Soundworks JSFX Pack
A collection of custom JSFX plugins for REAPER. Built for heavy music production — includes compressors, EQ utilities, metering tools, and more. Free and open source. Download the full pack from GitHub.

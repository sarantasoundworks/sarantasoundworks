About

Who I Am

I'm Burak — audio developer and metal music producer based in Turkey. I build DSP tools that run entirely in the browser, designed around the specific demands of extreme metal production.

The Saranta Mastering Suite is a full mastering-grade processing chain — parametric EQ, tape saturation, compression, stereo imaging, loudness metering — built from scratch with the Web Audio API. No DAW required, no install. Open the page, load your file, process the signal.
